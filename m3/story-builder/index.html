Story Builder Lab

Build a product story from an established requirement. The goal is not to guess a template answer; it is to preserve the requirement while making the user, need, and value visible.

Round 1 — Build the story

Validated requirement: The system shall provide feedback after a learner submits an answer.

Choose one option from each column. Your choices will be assembled into a story.

1. Who receives the value?

2. What capability is needed?

3. Why does it matter?

Transfer to your DataMan backlog

Open docs/requirements.md. Choose one validated requirement you expect to carry into your product backlog.

Before writing the full story, identify three things privately in your notes or directly in docs/product-backlog.md:

  • Who receives the value?
  • What capability or outcome is actually needed?
  • Why does that capability matter?

Then draft the story and preserve the requirement ID for traceability. You do not submit anything from this lab separately.
