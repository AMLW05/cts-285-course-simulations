Module 4 — From Product Work to Build-Ready Design

Work through this sequence in order. Each experience has a different job: practice the judgment, frame a question, operate a design, inspect consequences, synthesize evidence, and then carry a decision into the prototype.

Begin in Canvas

Complete the Module 4 Overview and Design for Implementation first. Those pages establish the ideas you will use here: implementation questions, evidence, interaction/behavior, data/state, architecture, traceability, and build readiness.

The sequence

1
Practice

Design Decisions Cabinet

Practice recognizing useful design questions, choosing evidence, placing responsibility in the right layer, testing models, and deciding whether work is ready.

Why first: this is controlled judgment practice before you make decisions about your own backlog slice.

Open Cabinet
2
MLO 4.1 · Frame

Design Investigation Brief

Select a Module 3 product slice. State the implementation uncertainty, identify the question type, and choose one or two evidence forms you expect may help.

Carry forward: save docs/design/m4-investigation-brief.md. This is your initial hypothesis, not your final answer.

Open Brief
3
MLO 4.2 · Operate

Answer Flow Lab

Configure the two-attempt behavior, operate learner scenarios, interrupt the session, inspect what survives, and revise until behavior, persistence, and layer responsibility work together.

Watch for: what a state flow or layer decision reveals that a static screen does not.

Open Lab
4
MLO 4.2 · Stress-test

Memory Bank & Curator Retrieval Lab

Build and stress a practice-set model, attempt an 11th problem, run learner activity, close the interface, and test whether a curator can retrieve specific missed problems later.

Watch for: whether your model can answer a real query after the interface is gone.

Open Lab
5
MLO 4.1–4.3 · Synthesize

DataMan Design Studio

Return to your Investigation Brief. Use what the controlled labs taught you to test your own product slice, inspect behavior/data/interface evidence, respond to a complication, and decide whether the work should proceed, revise, split, defer, block, or gather more evidence.

Carry forward: generate docs/decisions/m4-design-investigation-record.md.

Open Studio

Return to Canvas

Use your Investigation Brief, lab evidence, and Studio Findings in Design Investigation Application. That application captures the evidence and decision that matter for assessment.

Then: Design and Build Text-Only Prototype → text interface layer + reusable business logic layer + stubbed data layer.

What should change as you move through the sequence?

Your first question or planned evidence may be wrong. That is expected. The sequence is built so the controlled labs can challenge your initial plan before you commit to a build decision.

Question → Evidence → Decision → Consequence → Evaluation → Revision → Next Question
