Module 4 · Design Studio

DataMan Design Studio

Use representations as instruments. Your job is not to make every diagram. Your job is to discover what the product work still needs you to ask, know, see, test, or resolve before it is safe enough to build.

Question first. Evidence second. Decision third.

A flow can expose missing states. An ERD can expose data that cannot be stored or retrieved. A wireframe can expose ambiguity and dead ends. A small architecture sketch can expose responsibility in the wrong layer. The representation earns its place only if it helps answer the question.

An ERD answers storage and relationship questions

Entities are things the system must remember independently. Attributes describe them. Relationships explain how records connect. Cardinality forces you to say whether one record connects to one, many, or none of another.

Useful test: if two occurrences must remain distinguishable, be queried later, or carry their own facts, they may need separate records.
