Configure the design, run a learner through it, watch where it fails, then revise. The system behavior—not a form—tells you whether the design is build-ready.

Mission

The product slice must support exactly two attempts. After the first miss, the answer stays hidden and a second try is accepted. After the second miss, the correct answer is revealed. A curator must later be able to distinguish first-try success, second-try success, and two misses.

4. Articulate the decision
