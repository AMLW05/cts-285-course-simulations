DataMan Design Investigation Brief

Do not begin by choosing a diagram. Begin by deciding what your selected product work still needs you to understand.

What this activity does

This activity prepares you for the DataMan Design Studio. You will leave with a focused question and an evidence plan. You are not making the final design decision yet. The studio is where you create and inspect the evidence before deciding what should move forward.

1. Name the product work

Choose one story or a small connected story set from your Module 3 backlog.

2. What is still uncertain?

Choose the category that best describes the implementation question you need answered.

3. What evidence could answer it?

Select one or two evidence types you expect to use in the Design Studio. You may change your mind once you begin working.

Important: Choosing an ERD, wireframe, flow, or other representation here does not mean that representation is required for everyone. Your evidence choice should follow the question.

Three checks that may matter

Two attempts: Can the design distinguish try 1 from try 2 and support reveal after the second miss?

Memory Bank: Can the design represent or enforce the up-to-10-problem limit?

Results later: Can a curator retrieve a learner's past results after the session?

These checks are not equally relevant to every product slice. Use them when they apply.

Design principle

Simplicity: Use the minimum evidence needed to answer the question.

Feedback: Let the studio evidence challenge your first plan.

Courage: Change the question or evidence when your first approach is weak.
