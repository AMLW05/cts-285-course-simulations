# M4 Design Investigation Application Rubric — 12 points

Use whole points only. Performance levels: Mastery / Proficient / Developing / No Evidence.

| Criterion | Mastery — 3 | Proficient — 2 | Developing — 1 | No Evidence — 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Question + Evidence Selection** — MLO 4.1 | Frames an implementation-relevant question that is specific enough to investigate and selects/uses evidence that directly helps answer it; explains why the evidence was appropriate. | Frames a relevant question and uses generally appropriate evidence, with minor gaps in precision or rationale. | Question is broad, solution-first, or weakly connected to evidence; rationale is limited. | No usable design question or evidence selection is shown. |
| **Design Evidence + Traceability** — MLO 4.2 | Uses design evidence to clarify behavior, interaction, data/state, and/or architecture as appropriate; decisions trace clearly to requirements, acceptance criteria, backlog evidence, or observed lab consequences. | Uses design evidence that supports the decision and shows adequate traceability, with minor gaps. | Evidence is present but mostly descriptive, incomplete, or weakly traced to product work. | No meaningful design evidence or traceability is shown. |
| **Evaluation + Revision** — MLO 4.3 | Evaluates consequences, identifies a meaningful revision from initial thinking, names a tradeoff/unresolved uncertainty, and uses evidence to justify the change. | Shows evaluation and revision with generally sound reasoning, though one element may be underdeveloped. | Revision is asserted more than evaluated, or consequences/tradeoffs are vague. | No meaningful evaluation or revision is shown. |
| **Build-Readiness Decision + Next Question** — MLO 4.3 | Selects a defensible disposition (proceed/revise/split/gather evidence/block/defer), supports it with specific evidence, and identifies a productive next question. | Selects and supports a reasonable disposition; next question is relevant but may be broad. | Disposition is weakly supported, premature, or disconnected from the evidence; next question adds little. | No defensible readiness decision or next question is shown. |

## Alignment note
- MLO 4.1 is directly assessed in Criterion 1.
- MLO 4.2 is directly assessed in Criterion 2 and then applied again in the three-layer prototype.
- MLO 4.3 is directly assessed in Criteria 3–4.
