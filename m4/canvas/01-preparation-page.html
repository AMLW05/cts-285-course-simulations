MODULE 4 · PREPARE

Prepare for the DataMan Design Investigation

Module 4 moves from product work toward build-ready design. Before you begin the simulations, choose one product slice from your Module 3 backlog and identify what still needs to be understood before implementation should begin.

Governing question: What do we still need to ask, know, see, test, or resolve before this product work is safe and clear enough to build?

Before the simulations

  1. Complete the Design Decisions Cabinet. Use it to practice recognizing useful design questions, evidence, layer responsibility, and build-readiness decisions.
  2. Open the DataMan Design Investigation Brief.
  3. Select one story or a small connected story set from your Module 3 backlog.
  4. Identify one implementation-relevant uncertainty.
  5. Choose one or two evidence types that may help answer it.
  6. Save the generated brief in your repository as docs/design/m4-investigation-brief.md.

Do not solve the design yet

The Investigation Brief is a starting position. The operational labs may show that your original question was too broad, too narrow, or aimed at the wrong evidence. That is expected. Strong design work changes when evidence changes.

What comes next

You will test two controlled design problems before entering the Design Studio:

  • Answer Flow Lab: behavior, attempts, feedback, persistence, and reusable business logic.
  • Memory Bank & Curator Retrieval Lab: relationships, constraints, persistence, and later retrieval.

No Canvas submission is due on this page. Keep your brief available. You will use it again after the simulations.
