MODULE 4 · PREPARE

Prepare for the DataMan Design Investigation

You have already moved from evidence to requirements and from requirements to product work. Now the question changes: what still has to be understood before that work is safe and clear enough to build?

Module 4 governing question: What do we still need to ask, know, see, test, or resolve before this product work is safe and clear enough to build?

Your job before the labs

  1. Choose one product slice from your Module 3 backlog: one story or a small connected set of stories that represents meaningful work.
  2. Open the DataMan Design Investigation Brief when you reach it in the module.
  3. Identify one implementation-relevant uncertainty. Ask what the team still needs to understand about behavior, interaction, data/state, architecture, dependency, or readiness.
  4. Choose one or two kinds of evidence that could actually help answer that question.
  5. Save the resulting brief in your repository as docs/design/m4-investigation-brief.md.

Do not solve the design yet

The brief is your starting position, not your final answer. The next two labs are designed to challenge the way you are thinking about the problem. Your original question may survive. It may also narrow, expand, or change when the system exposes something you did not anticipate.

This mirrors professional systems work.

Analysts and developers rarely begin with every answer. They frame a useful question, select evidence, test assumptions, observe consequences, and revise before expensive implementation decisions harden into code.

What happens after the brief

You will work through two controlled simulations:

  • Answer Flow Lab — test attempt behavior, feedback, persistence, state, and where reusable rules belong.
  • Memory Bank & Curator Retrieval Lab — test relationships, constraints, persistence, and whether the design can answer a real retrieval need later.

In both labs, the goal is not to guess the instructor's preferred design. Make a decision, operate the system, inspect what happens, and revise when the evidence gives you a reason.

What to bring back to Canvas

When the labs are complete, return to the next Canvas page, What Did the System Make Visible? Bring your Investigation Brief and the important consequences you observed. You will preserve only the evidence that should influence your next design decision.

No Canvas submission is due on this page.
