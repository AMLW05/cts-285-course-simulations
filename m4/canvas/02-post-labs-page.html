MODULE 4 · AFTER THE LABS

What Did the System Make Visible?

You have now tested design decisions in two controlled DataMan situations. The goal was not to discover one perfect design. The goal was to see what different choices make possible, what they break, and what questions they force you to ask next.

Capture evidence, not a play-by-play

Use the M4 Lab Evidence Record to preserve only the findings that should influence later design work.

Look for changes in your thinking: Did either lab reveal a state, relationship, responsibility, constraint, or retrieval need that your Investigation Brief did not account for?

Before entering the Design Studio

  1. Record the important failure or near-failure from each lab.
  2. Record the revision that changed the consequence.
  3. Identify the design principle you would carry into another interface or implementation.
  4. Return to your original Investigation Brief.
  5. Write the question you would ask now.

Your original question may stay the same. It may also narrow, broaden, or change completely. The important part is that the change is supported by evidence.

No separate Canvas submission is due here. Keep the Lab Evidence Record with your Module 4 work. You will use it in the Design Studio and in the graded application.
