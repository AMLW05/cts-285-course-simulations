MODULE 4 · DESIGN STUDIO

Investigate Your Product Slice

The controlled labs gave you known situations to test. The Design Studio changes the job: use those same reasoning lenses on your own selected product slice.

Bring forward: your m4-investigation-brief.md, your lab evidence, the relevant Module 2 requirements/traceability, and your Module 3 backlog/acceptance criteria.

This is not another pass through the labs

You already practiced two-attempt behavior, persistence, set constraints, and curator retrieval in controlled situations. In the Studio, decide which representation or test will help answer the question for your product work.

You may use:

  • a behavior or state flow;
  • a wireframe or interaction sketch;
  • an ERD or relationship fragment;
  • a field/state table;
  • a sequence or layer/component sketch;
  • an acceptance-criteria trace;
  • a dependency/constraint check;
  • a small structural spike;
  • another representation that directly answers the question.

You are not required to make all of these. Use the minimum evidence needed to make the decision defensible.

Leave the Studio with a decision

Your Studio work should allow you to complete docs/decisions/m4-design-investigation-record.md and decide whether the selected work should:

proceed · revise · split · gather more evidence · block/return for clarification · defer

After the Studio, return to Canvas for the graded Design Investigation Application.
