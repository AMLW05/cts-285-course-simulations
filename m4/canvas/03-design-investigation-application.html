MODULE 4 · APPLICATION

Design Investigation Application

Use your Investigation Brief, operational lab evidence, and Design Studio findings to make a build-readiness decision for one product slice from your Module 3 backlog.

This is not a diagram checklist. Submit the evidence that answers your design question and supports your decision. Different product slices may require different evidence.

Your submission

Submit a link to the following file in your CTS-285 GitHub repository:

docs/decisions/m4-design-investigation-record.md

Your record must include:

  • the selected product slice and trace to requirement(s), acceptance criteria, or backlog evidence;
  • the initial design question and the question after controlled lab testing;
  • the evidence actually used and why it was useful;
  • findings about interaction/behavior, data/state, and architecture/responsibility as applicable;
  • the design decision and its consequence;
  • a revision from your initial thinking;
  • one tradeoff and any unresolved uncertainty;
  • a build-readiness disposition: proceed, revise, split, gather more evidence, block/return for clarification, or defer;
  • the next question the team should ask.

Evidence may include

A state/behavior flow, ERD fragment, wireframe, sequence representation, layer sketch, field/state table, acceptance-criteria trace, dependency check, or another representation that materially helped answer the question. Do not add an artifact only because it appears in this list.

What happens next

Your approved/revised design decision becomes the basis for the Design and Build Text-Only Prototype. The prototype will test whether the behavior, responsibility, and data decisions can survive a three-layer implementation structure.

Submit

In Canvas, submit the direct GitHub link to m4-design-investigation-record.md. Do not upload duplicate screenshots or a second copy of the record unless your instructor specifically requests one.
