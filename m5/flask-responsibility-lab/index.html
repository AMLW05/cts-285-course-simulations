Flask Responsibility Lab

Place responsibilities in a small DataMan Flask flow, run the design, inspect what your choice makes easier or harder, and revise before you touch your own project.

Goal: not to memorize a folder structure. Your job is to decide which responsibility belongs to Flask, reusable business logic, or the data/state boundary—and to notice the consequences when those boundaries blur.

Framework / Interface

Receive request data, call application behavior, select the next response or template.

Business Logic

Apply DataMan rules independently of one specific user interface.

Data / State

Retrieve or preserve the information that the behavior depends on.
